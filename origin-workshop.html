How points and sharing work
  • The draft starts at 10 points. Powers spend points and meaningful weaknesses earn refunds, currently capped at 6. Both limits are open for discussion.
  • Some sliders can become weaknesses. Larger boosts and shorter cooldowns cost more.
  • Refunds reflect what remains after powers offset a weakness. Movement, food, healing, fire and shield weaknesses each receive only their strongest eligible refund. Costs round up and refunds round down to tenths.
  • Copy your build to share it, or download a build file and load it here later.
  • Use Custom to propose your own powers, weaknesses, or other ideas. They stay unpriced until discussed with the server owner.
  • Catalogue choices are up for discussion too. Use Discussion notes for different prices, strengths, timers, combinations, or restrictions.
  • The server owner and player agree on the final design before it becomes available in-game. PvP powers must respect server rules and protected areas.
How combinations change refunds

Keep any trait for your character, even when its refund becomes zero. The builder explains adjusted refunds below each selected trait and in the shared summary.

  • Fire immunity removes sunlight-burning points. Photosynthesis removes diet and hunger-related refunds.
  • Speed and melee boosts remove the matching losses in environmental weaknesses. Gliding and active travel reduce ground penalties.
  • Origin healing removes healing-source refunds. Longer regeneration potions reduce Weak healing potions.
  • Natural armor reduces equipment-restriction refunds. Missing required powers and incompatible substitutes earn no bonus points.
  • Overlapping weaknesses in one family earn their strongest eligible refund, rather than several refunds for avoiding the same system.
  • Extra durability wear earns no automatic refund while Mending is allowed. Tool dependence and Cannot sleep are unpriced flavor restrictions.
  • Vision weaknesses: Night vision or Soul lantern removes their automatic refunds pending final shader/stacking tests. Clear underwater vision removes Murky underwater vision's refund; Focus zoom removes Nearsightedness's. Detection aids halve refunds. Only the strongest remaining vision-weakness refund counts. A zero-refund weakness stays selected and still needs implementation review. Equivalent potions, goggles, shaders or custom powers that bypass it earn no refund.

These are conservative discussion rules. Custom powers, equipment, farms, server settings and other players' buffs may change the real burden; agree final refunds for the actual modpack.
